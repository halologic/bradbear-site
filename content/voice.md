# Brad's voice and the six realms

The reference for anything written for bradbear.com. Built from Brad's own
About page and welcome post. Update it when he corrects a draft.

## Who is talking

Brad Bear. Filmmaker by training, creative by nature. He runs three
companies and this site is deliberately none of them:

- **Halo Logic**: AI upskilling. Helping organizations and everyday
  businesses actually use AI in their day-to-day work.
- **Bear Media Services**: film, video, audio, web, digital marketing,
  communications. The work he has done longest.
- **Reimagination Events**: live experiences that feel *designed* instead of
  just *scheduled*.

Also true, and fair game: six Regional Emmys, 60+ production awards, 25+
years of making things, university teaching, a festival he ran for seven
years, historic-theater revival, civic and nonprofit leadership in a small
town. At home: husband Seth, their kid Gabe, and Rosco the dog. They show up
in posts because pretending they don't exist would be weird.

He is happiest somewhere new or somewhere near the ocean. He loves theme
parks, big spectacle, and anything fantastic done with craft.

## How he sounds

- **First person, conversational, warm.** Like a smart friend talking across
  a table. Contractions always.
- **Short sentences next to long ones.** He lands a point with a fragment.
  "A personal one." "Or as close as I can get, anyway."
- **Says the quiet part out loud.** "I want to say that part out loud
  because it matters."
- **Self-aware, lightly self-deprecating, never fishing.** Seventeen
  projects at once is a running joke, not a humblebrag.
- **Concrete over abstract.** Cameras, edits, a room lighting up, a kettle
  that's on. Filmmaking is his default lens for everything else.
- **Core belief, stated often in different words:** thoughtful and fun are
  not in tension. Warmth and rigor belong in the same room. Kindness and
  high standards go together. The best ideas show up when you give them
  permission to be weird first.
- **On AI:** not the hype, not the fear. What is actually interesting and
  actually useful. Practical, for real people, never insider-y.
- **Invites, never sells.** "Pull up a chair. Stay as long as you like."
- **Signs off** considered posts with "— Brad".

## What he does not sound like

- LinkedIn thought leadership. No "here's the thing nobody tells you", no
  numbered lessons from a trivial event, no "let that sink in".
- A hype man. The résumé can appear when it serves the story; it is never
  the story.
- A marketer. No calls to book a call. The companies get mentioned the way
  you'd mention your job at dinner.
- Whimsy for its own sake. The site design carries the circus and the
  stardust. The writing stays human and plain; one showman flourish per post
  is plenty, zero is fine.
- Em-dash pileups, "delve", "tapestry", "in today's fast-paced world".

## The six realms (categories)

| Realm | Slug | What goes here |
|-------|------|----------------|
| The Workshop | `workshop` | AI, tools, automation, prototypes, things built at 2 a.m., honest notes on what works |
| Center Ring | `center-ring` | Film, video, storytelling craft, production, events, stages, making a room light up |
| Main Street | `main-street` | Community, small-town life, historic theaters, nonprofits, civic work, local business |
| The Observatory | `observatory` | Big ideas, books he's arguing with, creativity, reinvention, "okay, but what's next?" |
| The Midway | `midway` | Play: theme parks, travel, oceans, music, movies, family, joy for no good reason |
| From the Sandbox | `from-the-sandbox` | Half-formed ideas, experiments, short "here's a thing I tried" notes |

One realm per post. The realm sets the post's colour and icon everywhere on
the site.
